3. Practical differences

ProviderNotes that matter commercially
AWSLargest ecosystem and the easiest to hire for. A simplified bundled plan (Lightsail) can cut this to roughly ₹2,800 per month if managed database features are not needed.
Google CloudCheapest of the three at this size and the simplest billing. Sustained-use discounts apply automatically, with no commitment needed.
AzureMost expensive here, but the natural choice if the institute already runs Microsoft 365 or has Azure credits through an education agreement.

4. Points to note before deciding

  • All three offer first-year free credits of roughly US$ 200 to 300, so year one may cost noticeably less than shown.
  • Educational institutions can often apply for additional non-profit or academic credits from all three providers.
  • These figures cover infrastructure only. They do not include anyone's time to manage it.
  • Costs rise with usage. Adding institutes, students or heavy reporting increases the bill; the on-premise option does not change.
  • Data leaves the premises. Confirm this is acceptable for your compliance requirements.
Summary. Cloud costs roughly ₹5,000 to ₹6,200 a month with no upfront spend, and gives you resilience, backups and scale without owning hardware. Over five years that is about ₹3.1 to ₹3.7 lakh, compared with roughly ₹47,000 for the on-premise Raspberry Pi option. Cloud is the better fit if uptime guarantees matter, if the institute has no on-site technical staff, or if you expect to grow well beyond a few hundred users.
