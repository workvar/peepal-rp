4. What you gain

  • No monthly cloud bill. Costs are known and fixed.
  • All institutional data stays physically on your premises.
  • No per-user or per-record charges as the institute grows.
  • Cloudflare Tunnel publishes the site securely at no cost, with no static IP needed.
  • Pays for itself against cloud within roughly five months.

5. What you take on

  • If the building loses power or internet beyond the UPS window, the system is offline.
  • One machine, so a hardware failure means downtime until it is replaced.
  • Someone on staff must confirm that backups are running.
  • Comfortable for a few hundred users. Beyond roughly 500 concurrent users, move to cloud or a larger server.
Recommended for this client. At 200 to 300 users the workload is small, the cost difference is large, and the trade-offs above are manageable. A second identical Pi kept as a cold spare adds about ₹9,000 and removes most of the hardware risk.
